# RUINBREAKER — Season 1 Series Bible

**Format:** TV anime, 12 episodes × ~24 min (Episode 1 is an 11½-minute voiced, animated episode in `anime/index.html`)
**Genre:** Isekai · dark fantasy · monster hunting
**Logline:** A night-shift kid from Tokyo falls through a hole in the sky into a world where only the Branded can fight the monsters, and he pulls out an axe that nobody else could move in three hundred years. Something lives inside it.

---

## The world

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Ashen March** | The frontier of the other world. Graveyards, ruins and Rifts. |
| **Rifts** | Cracks in the sky. Fiends crawl out of them. Rifts open upward and never sideways, until Episode 1. |
| **Fiends** | Monsters from the Rifts. Ranked **Lesser → Greater → Calamity**. Ordinary weapons barely scratch them. |
| **Brand** | An ability carved into the soul. It shows up as a glowing mark on the body and only awakens when the bearer is close to death. Without a Brand you cannot hurt a Fiend, and you cannot become a Hunter. |
| **Hunters** | Branded people licensed by the **Hunters' Guild**. Ranked F → S. |
| **Skyfallen** | People from Earth who fell through a Rift. They are rare, they are never sent home, and the Guild doesn't like to talk about them. |
| **The Gilded Order** | A church-backed Hunter order in the golden capital, **Saint Lumen**. |

## Season 1 episode guide

1. **The Crossing.** 2:14 a.m., Nerima. Takuto is feeding the cat that used to belong to his missing friend when the sky cracks open over the railway crossing. A cat-eared girl falls out, and a Skeletal Shieldbearer follows her. Both of them get pulled through the Rift. In the Barrow Fields, Takuto pulls the Headsman's Axe out of its grave. When Nemu dies protecting him, a voice inside the axe asks him what he wants to break.
2. **Branded.** Sieg escorts Takuto and Nemu to the frontier town of Ashford and the Hunters' Guild. Valentine's cube Tofu reads Takuto's rank as ERROR. Rin Ashveil challenges him on sight, and Takuto learns the hard way that Ruinbreaker cannot block.
3. **A Flame From Home.** At the licensing exam in the Bramble Wastes, Takuto is forced to team up with Rin. He meets Wick, another Skyfallen, who tells him nobody has ever gone home. Then the Stonehide Behemoth wakes up.
4. **Cogwheel Heart.** Gideon builds Takuto a chain harness so the axe can block exactly once. Someone sabotages the Guild forge, and a man in a black suit is seen smiling in the smoke.
5. **Seven Heads, One River.** In the river town of Marrowmere, the Hydra regrows every head Takuto cuts off. Breaking a thing and killing it turn out to be different problems, and Rin's threads become part of the answer.
6. **The Red Giants March.** A Red Giant raid on Fort Cinder. Takuto loses control for the first time and Akuro's eye opens. Rin gets hurt because of it.
7. **Feathers and Ash.** The Plume Ogres attack a village, but they are running from something. Takuto learns that some Fiends can be reasoned with, and that some of the Fiends are being herded.
8. **The Fang Ronin.** Kurozane challenges Sieg to a duel and Sieg's past catches up with him. Afterimage against the wolf's blade.
9. **Saint Lumen.** In the golden capital, Lucien Aurel invites Takuto to join the Gilded Order. Takuto finds Haruki standing at Lucien's side. Haruki doesn't want to be rescued.
10. **Morvudd.** Sieg's nemesis returns with a horde. Sieg nearly dies holding the line. Mr. Sable finally shows himself to Takuto: "I'm the one who brought you here."
11. **Calamity.** Tyr the Frost Titan is unleashed on Saint Lumen. To save the city, Takuto lets Akuro take over and becomes the Crimson Calamity. Vesper is ordered to execute him. Nemu reaches him by spending her eighth life.
12. **Ruinbreaker.** Lucien raises a Sanctum over the whole city to harvest every Brand inside it. Haruki freezes Lucien for three seconds, which is all Takuto needs to break the unbreakable barrier. Sable escapes. A Rift to Earth flickers open, and Takuto chooses to stay. *Post-credits:* Mr. Sable stands at the Nerima crossing as the bell starts ringing.

How Episode 1 is made:
- **Voices:** every line is voiced with the open-source Kokoro-82M speech model (Apache-2.0). Each character has their own blended voice; Akuro's is pitched down with a sub-octave layer, and Mr. Sable's carries static. Mouths and jaws move with the voice. Voice script: `voice/lines.json`; generator: `tools/make_voices.py`.
- **Animation:** characters are rigged as deformable puppets (`js/rig.js`, `js/rigs.js`). They breathe, blink, glance around and look at whoever they're talking to (irises move inside the eyes and the face turns), talk, swing their arms and weapons, and walk, with hair, coats, ribbons and tails swaying.
- **Frame rate:** a new frame is drawn on every screen refresh (60, 90, 100, 120 or 144 fps depending on the display), and resolution adjusts automatically to hold that speed. The player shows the live fps.
- **Missing parts:** Takuto's art stops at the waist and Sieg's and Mr. Sable's at the thighs, so their legs and boots are drawn in code (`js/parts.js`). Takuto's crescent axe and Sieg's glaive were cut off by the edges of the art, so both are redrawn complete as separate parts that swing from the hand.
- **Fight cuts:** full-body action is staged as backlit silhouettes against a giant moon (`js/fighter.js`): figures with real joints run, leap, flip, duck and swing, with hit-stops, slow motion and afterimages, intercut with close-ups of the original art.
- **Effects:** GPU post-processing (bloom, colour grading, chromatic aberration, shockwave distortion, zoom blur) plus energy slashes, flame auras, sparks, debris, light shafts and holographic Brand readouts (`js/post.js`, `js/vfx.js`).
- **Sound:** the score, ambience and sound effects are synthesized live in the browser.
